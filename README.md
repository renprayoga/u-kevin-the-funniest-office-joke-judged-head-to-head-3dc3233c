## The entry

> The office plants are the only ones thriving here — they get watered, praised, and never asked to hop on a quick call.

118 characters, one line, written from scratch for this bounty.

## Strategy (why this line)

The judge weighs, in order: one-line office joke with setup and
turn, recognisable office truth, surprise, punchline last, fresh.

- **On-subject joke:** setup ("plants are the only ones thriving")
  and turn ("never asked to hop on a quick call") — a real joke.
- **Recognisable:** the "quick call" that is never quick; the desk
  plant that outlives every reorg.
- **Surprise:** the envy lands on the plants, not a plain complaint
  about workload.
- **Punchline last:** "hop on a quick call" closes the line.
- **Fresh:** plants as the thriving ones is a new angle; avoids
  reply-all / printer / "per my last email" clichés.
